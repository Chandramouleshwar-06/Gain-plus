# Gain+ Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

Gain+ ("the app") is a workout, nutrition and activity tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and the choices you have.

## Summary

- Everything you log is stored **on your phone**. The app works fully without an account.
- If you **choose** to sign in, your data is backed up to your own private space in Google Firebase so it syncs between your devices.
- Health data from Health Connect is only read **if you turn it on**. It is used only to show your stats and log your activities in the app.
- The app has **no ads, no analytics and no tracking**, and your data is never sold or shared for advertising.

## Data stored on your phone

Workouts, sets, plans, programs, schedules, exercises, goals, body weight, body measurements, food logs, activities, your profile (name, height, year of birth, sex, activity level, goal, equipment) and app settings are stored in a local database on your phone.

**Progress photos** are stored only on your phone. They are never uploaded.

## Optional account and sync (Google Firebase)

If you sign in with Google or with email and password:

- **Firebase Authentication** (Google LLC) stores your email address and, for Google sign-in, your Google account's basic profile, so you can sign in.
- **Cloud Firestore** (Google LLC, hosted in Mumbai, India) stores a copy of the data listed above (except progress photos), so it can sync between your devices.
- Only you can read or write your data. Access is restricted to your signed-in account.
- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by Google.

Signing out uploads your changes, then removes your data from that phone.

## Health Connect (Android)

If you connect Health Connect, the app asks for permission to:

- **Read:** steps, active calories burned, total calories burned, distance, exercise sessions, weight, heart rate, resting heart rate and sleep.
- **Write:** exercise sessions, with their distance and calories burned.

How this data is used:

- It is used only to show your daily stats (steps, calories, heart rate, sleep) in the app.
- Workouts recorded by other apps and devices are added to your activities.
- Your latest weight is added to your body weight log.
- Workouts you log in Gain+ are saved to Health Connect.

Activities and weights imported from Health Connect become part of your Gain+ data. They are stored and synced as described above. Your other health data (steps, heart rate and sleep) is only shown in the app and not stored or uploaded.

Health Connect data is **not** used for advertising, sold, shared with third parties or used to decide your eligibility for credit, insurance or employment. The app's use of Health Connect data follows the [Health Connect Permissions policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12991134), including its Limited Use requirements.

You can turn off access at any time in Gain+ (Profile → Health Connect → Disconnect) or in the Health Connect app.

## Other services

- **Food search (Open Food Facts):** when you search for a packaged food, the search text is sent to [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org) to find matching products. No personal data is sent with it.
- **Exercise demos (YouTube):** "Watch a demo" opens a YouTube search in the YouTube app or your browser. YouTube's own privacy policy applies there.
- **Notifications:** reminders are scheduled on your phone; no notification service is used.

## Permissions

- **Notifications and exact alarms:** workout reminders and the rest timer.
- **Internet:** sign-in, sync and food search.
- **Camera and photos:** only when you add a progress photo. Photos stay on your phone.
- **Health Connect:** only if you connect it (see above).

## Keeping and deleting your data

- **Local data:** this stays on your phone until you delete it, sign out or uninstall the app.
- **Synced data:** this is kept until you delete your account.
- **Delete your account in the app:** go to Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your synced data and your sign-in, and removes your data from that phone.
- **Request deletion without the app:** email the address below from your account's email address, asking for your account to be deleted. It will be done within 30 days.

## Children

Gain+ is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
